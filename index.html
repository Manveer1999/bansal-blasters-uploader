Bansal Blasters Uploader

A private tool for one YouTube channel

Bansal Blasters Uploader is a personal, non-public tool used by Manveer Singh to publish videos to his own YouTube channel, Bansal Blasters.

What it does

  • Edits videos locally on the owner's computer.
  • Uploads the finished video, its title, description, tags and thumbnail to the owner's channel using the YouTube Data API (videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
  • Reads the channel's own video status and public channel statistics (videos.list, channels.list).

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. There are no other users, no sign-up, and it is not offered to the public. Every upload is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is sent.
